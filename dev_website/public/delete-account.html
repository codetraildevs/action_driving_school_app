Delete Your Account

Follow these steps to permanently remove your Rwanda Traffic Rule account and all associated data.

How to Delete Your Account

1

Open the App

Launch Rwanda Traffic Rule on your Android device and sign in to your account.

2

Go to Your Profile

Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.

Home›Profile
3

Tap “Delete Account”

Scroll down to find the Delete Account button, displayed in red at the bottom of the Profile page.

Profile›Delete Account
4

Confirm Deletion

A confirmation dialog will appear. Tap “Delete” to permanently remove your account. This action cannot be undone.

Deletion Timeline

Immediately

You are signed out and can no longer access your account. Local cached data is cleared from the device.

Within 30 Days

All personal data is permanently erased from our servers. This includes your name, phone number, device ID hash, exam results, and subscription records.

After 30 Days

No trace of your account exists in our systems. The deletion is irreversible.

Important Notes

⚠️ If you have an active subscription, it will not be refunded for the remaining period. Consider using your subscription before deleting.

💡 You can create a new account with the same phone number after deletion, but all previous progress will be lost.
